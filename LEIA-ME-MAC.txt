A2R LINK 0.1.5 - MAC
Do Premiere e do After Effects direto para o DaVinci Resolve, com um clique. Sem exportar timeline.

O QUE PRECISA
- Mac (Apple Silicon ou Intel)
- DaVinci Resolve STUDIO (a versao gratuita nao serve)
- Premiere Pro e/ou After Effects
- Python 3.14, de python.org (botao Downloads > macOS)

INSTALAR
1. Duplo clique em INSTALAR.command.
   Se o macOS bloquear com "desenvolvedor nao identificado" ou "nao pode ser aberto":
   clique com o BOTAO DIREITO no arquivo > Abrir > Abrir. Pronto, e so isso.
   (Se mesmo assim nao abrir: abra o Terminal, arraste INSTALAR.command para dentro
   dele, aperte Enter.)
2. No DaVinci: DaVinci Resolve > Settings... > System > General >
   "External scripting using" = Local. Reinicie o DaVinci.
3. Reinicie o Premiere e o After Effects.

USAR
1. Abra o DaVinci Resolve Studio com um projeto aberto.
2. No Premiere ou no After: menu Janela > Extensoes > A2R Link.
3. Selecione os clipes (Premiere) ou as camadas de video (After) e clique em "Enviar ao DaVinci".
   Sem selecao, vai tudo da sequencia/comp ativa.
4. No DaVinci aparece: bin com o nome do projeto > sub-bin com o nome da sequencia/comp > a midia e a timeline.
   Reenviar nao sobrescreve: cria "Nome 2".

O QUE VAI: Anchor Point, Position, Scale, Rotation, Opacity, keyframes e curvas, Flip, corte, trilhas, velocidade.
O QUE NAO VAI: efeitos e plugins, track mattes, mascaras, parenting, expressoes (3D vira 2D).

DEU ERRO?
O cartao vermelho explica em portugues e ja gera um relatorio na hora.
Clique em "Relatorio para o suporte": a pasta abre com o arquivo A2R-debug-*.zip
selecionado. Mande esse zip para o suporte junto com um print.
Causas mais comuns: DaVinci fechado, sem projeto aberto, ou "External scripting" fora de Local.

DESINSTALAR
Duplo clique em DESINSTALAR.command (mesmo esquema: botao direito > Abrir se bloquear).
Remove os paineis, o script do DaVinci e a configuracao.
As timelines e bins ja criados no DaVinci ficam (sao seus).

===== ENGLISH =====

A2R LINK 0.1.5 - MAC
From Premiere and After Effects straight into DaVinci Resolve, one click. No timeline export.

REQUIREMENTS
- Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel)
- DaVinci Resolve STUDIO (the free version does not work)
- Premiere Pro and/or After Effects
- Python 3.14, from python.org (Downloads button > macOS)

INSTALL
1. Double-click INSTALAR.command.
   If macOS blocks it with "unidentified developer" or "cannot be opened":
   RIGHT-CLICK the file > Open > Open. That is all.
   (If it still will not open: open Terminal, drag INSTALAR.command into it, press Enter.)
2. In DaVinci: DaVinci Resolve > Settings... > System > General >
   "External scripting using" = Local. Restart DaVinci.
3. Restart Premiere and After Effects.

USE
1. Open DaVinci Resolve Studio with a project open.
2. In Premiere or After: Window > Extensions > A2R Link.
3. Select the clips (Premiere) or video layers (After) and click "Send to DaVinci".
   With nothing selected, everything from the active sequence/comp is sent.
4. In DaVinci you get: a bin named after the project > a sub-bin named after the sequence/comp > media and timeline.
   Resending does not overwrite: it creates "Name 2".

WHAT IS SENT: Anchor Point, Position, Scale, Rotation, Opacity, keyframes and curves, Flip, trim, tracks, speed.
WHAT IS NOT: effects and plugins, track mattes, masks, parenting, expressions (3D becomes 2D).

IF SOMETHING FAILS
The red card explains the problem and creates a report on the spot.
Click "Support report": the folder opens with the A2R-debug-*.zip file selected.
Send that zip to support along with a screenshot.
Most common causes: DaVinci closed, no project open, or "External scripting" not set to Local.

UNINSTALL
Double-click DESINSTALAR.command (same deal: right-click > Open if blocked).
Removes the panels, the DaVinci script and the configuration.
Timelines and bins already created in DaVinci stay (they are yours).
